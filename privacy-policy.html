Privacy Policy

How Fineyesse handles your data — clearly and honestly.

🔒

The short version: Your financial data is stored only in your own Google account. We never see it, never store it on our servers, and never share it with anyone. SMS is read on-device only and never transmitted anywhere.

01
Information We Collect

Fineyesse does not collect, store, or transmit any personal or financial data to our servers. The app operates entirely on your device and your personal Google account.

  • We do not maintain any database of user information
  • We do not collect usage analytics or behavioural data
  • We do not track your location
  • We do not use advertising identifiers
02
Google Account Access

Fineyesse uses Google Sign-In for authentication and requests access to Google Sheets and Google Drive to create and manage your personal "Cashflow Tracker" spreadsheet.

This spreadsheet is created in your own Google Drive. It belongs to you. We have no access to it at any time. You can view, edit, or delete it directly from Google Drive whenever you wish.

  • Data is written to your Google account via encrypted HTTPS
  • We request only the minimum permissions required
  • You can revoke access at any time via your Google account settings
03
SMS Access

Fineyesse requests permission to read SMS messages on your device to automatically detect bank transactions. This is the core functionality of the app.

  • We read only SMS messages from known bank sender IDs (e.g. HDFCBK, ICICIB, SBIPSG)
  • Personal SMS messages are never read or processed
  • SMS content is processed entirely on your device
  • No SMS content is ever transmitted to any server
  • Parsed transaction data is saved only to your personal Google Sheet
04
Data Storage & Security

All your financial data is stored exclusively in your personal Google Sheet in your Google Drive account. Fineyesse has no server-side storage of any kind.

  • Zero server-side storage — we have no database
  • All communication with Google APIs uses encrypted HTTPS
  • Biometric authentication protects app access on your device
  • Screenshot prevention is enabled to protect your financial data
05
Third Party Services

Fineyesse uses the following third party services:

  • Google Sign-In — for authentication. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Sheets API — for storing your financial data in your own account
  • Google Drive API — for creating and managing your spreadsheet

No other third party services, analytics tools, or advertising networks are used.

06
Children's Privacy

Fineyesse is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the app, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

07
Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with an updated date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
